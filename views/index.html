The Women Behind ELATEVE

ELATEVE is a group of women in their 30s, 40s, 50s and 60s. Each of us hit the same wall — our own burnout, our own hormonal upheaval, recovery that standard procedures met with a shrug, and a curiosity that nobody around us would feed.

Our backgrounds are in the luxury and lifestyle world across Barcelona, Paris and London, and in building businesses over decades. Between us we have navigated postpartum recovery, perimenopause, menopause and the years beyond — and the quiet confusion of not knowing which protocol, product or professional to trust.

So we compare notes, we disagree about what actually works, and we test everything on ourselves before it ever reaches you. If we wouldn’t put it in our own bodies, or recommend it to our own mothers, sisters and daughters, it doesn’t make the cut.

✦

Kloodos — Our Technology Partner

Kloodos is a female-led family business, and has been the wellness-technology curator to elite sport, luxury hospitality and private clinics since 2014 — started by a founder who came to it with exactly the same questions we did.

Behind it: more than 30 years in the spa and wellness industry, award-winning treatment protocols, collaboration with Premier League sports-science departments, and distribution agreements with the world’s leading recovery-technology manufacturers. Whole Body Cryotherapy, Photobiomodulation, Hyperbaric Oxygen, NAD+ and Vitamin IV, medical compression, dry float and more — clinically validated, medical-grade, and sequenced into protocols rather than sold as a room of machines.
